# Housing empirical coverage — acquired November 10, 2026

Two complete original Statistics Canada bulk tables were downloaded: 65,880 NHPI observations and 343,980 construction observations. `process.py` preserves the original files, filters Ontario geographies from December 2016, retains components and status flags, rejects duplicate keys, and produces 3,894 NHPI rows and 26,550 construction rows. Both panels end in September 2026. NHPI has 10 Ontario CMAs plus the province; construction has 15 Ontario CMAs. There are no missing values in the retained panels, but this does not imply comprehensive coverage of housing markets. Barrie has construction data but no NHPI series in this table.

NHPI measures the change in agreed builder selling prices before GST/HST for new singles, semis and townhouses, with specifications held constant between consecutive observations. It is an index, not the dollar price of a typical home, and excludes condos. Raw values are not seasonally adjusted. Construction includes starts, completions and units under construction for five dwelling-type groups. Units under construction are not unsold inventory. Census geography revisions limit strict historical comparability.

Primary links: [NHPI table](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1810020501), [NHPI September 2026 definitions](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261022/dq261022d-eng.htm), [construction table](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3410015401).

Builder evidence includes a downloaded three-page Fieldgate price sheet, printed July 11, 2026, with 48 model/elevation offer rows; 33 show conditional advertised rebates. All 33 subtraction identities pass. The URL's AUG-12 filename is not used to override the printed date. A downloaded Tamarack page supplies one unit-specific current asking-price snapshot. Four historical Tamarack unit prices are available only in a search-index extraction of a November 12, 2025 sheet: direct download returns HTTP 404. They are explicitly quarantined as unverified archival leads. The candidate 131 Kerala pair also has a 2,285-versus-2,270 square-foot discrepancy and unknown current price effective date. There is therefore **no verified matched longitudinal transaction-price panel** and no credible policy-effect estimate.

Eight of nine attempted original HTTP sources downloaded successfully. The original supply and demand research PDFs, NHPI methodology and Mattamy price-basis disclosure are retained. `source_register.csv`, `raw/acquisition_log.json`, and `qa.json` supply paths, timestamps, hashes, definitions and exceptions.

Not acquired: sales/absorption or unsold-inventory time series, local permit/zoning/approval microdata, matched transaction prices, harmonized local demand instruments, or a historical no-policy comparator. These are gaps, not zeros. This is a substantial pilot acquisition, not an exhaustive census of all potentially public evidence.
