# Supported empirical findings

In September 2026, the Ontario total-house-and-land NHPI was 116.9 (December 2016=100), versus 121.0 a year earlier: a calculated decline of 3.39%. Toronto fell 4.89%, Hamilton 3.97%, Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo 2.02%, and London 1.94%; Ottawa's Ontario portion rose 1.00%, and Greater Sudbury rose 2.51%. These are changes in published tax-exclusive price indexes, not estimates of buyers' rebate benefit. See `processed/nhpi_latest_comparison.csv` and [Statistics Canada](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1810020501).

The [Fieldgate price sheet](https://www.fieldgatehomes.com/upper-east/brokerportal/files/PriceList/UES-NEW-RELEASE-PRICE-LIST-AUG-12.pdf), printed July 11, 2026, offers a direct illustration of advertised price-basis ambiguity: Sprucegrove A lists a $1,499,900 base, a $106,000 proposed-government-rebate deduction and a $1,393,900 after-rebate price. Those are simultaneous advertised columns, **not prices observed at three different dates**, and the $106,000 must not be presented as additional savings from the last figure. Thirty-three of the 48 offer rows print such a deduction; the other 15 leave the rebate unreported. These are builder claims subject to eligibility, not independently recalculated tax entitlements.

[Mattamy's disclosure](https://mattamyhomes.com/promos/government-rebates) states that its Ontario website prices include an estimated enhanced rebate subject to eligibility. This reinforces the need to separate advertised prices, tax-exclusive prices, rebate assignment and final cash payable.

Published literature supports heterogeneity in historical supply responsiveness and a distinct national demand parameter. It does not supply matched local new-home supply and demand estimates for this policy. The available observations therefore do **not** support a numerical claim about the percentage of Ontario relief captured by developers, or a regional ranking of buyer capture. Absence of an identified estimate is not evidence of zero capture.
