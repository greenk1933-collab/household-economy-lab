# Attempted sources and remaining evidence gaps

- StatCan bulk downloads initially failed with local DNS restrictions. The root agent obtained network permission and downloaded both originals successfully. No proxy or access-control bypass was used.
- The old Tamarack November 12, 2025 PDF remains indexed in web search, but direct original download returned HTTP 404. Four extracted historical prices are marked `unverified archival lead`. They cannot establish a verified matched panel.
- Tamarack's current 131 Kerala page exceeded the web-reader content limit; the original HTTP HTML download succeeded. Its page-modified timestamp is not treated as the time its price changed.
- Fieldgate's public Upper East broker portal links to a scanned PDF. Text extraction returned no meaningful text. PDFium rendering and visual transcription succeeded. No authentication was needed. The sheet's three pages provide one observation date, not a history.
- Searches for Minto price lists, Mattamy historic price packages, Fieldgate price lists and Tamarack historic prices found leads; the selected primary documents are in the register. Third-party portals requiring free accounts were not entered or bypassed.
- No complete archive/CDX historical extraction was run. No invented snapshots are substituted. There is no verified treated/untreated transaction panel.
- Supply literature was found in Bank of Canada SAN2021-21; national demand literature in WP2015-40. No independently verified matching city-level demand coefficients or uncertainty intervals were obtained. Newer Bank of Canada monetary-shock papers are identified as follow-up instruments research, not numerical local parameters.
- The construction table is not an unsold-inventory dataset. Sales, absorption, unsold inventory, zoning/approval times, population/income controls and matched unit tax treatment remain unacquired in this pilot.
