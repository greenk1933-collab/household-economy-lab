## Requested outputs constrained by unavailable evidence

- **Matched longitudinal property prices:** no verified pre/post panel was acquired. Four historical leads are quarantined, not promoted as observations. Actual regional index history and current builder offers are supplied; there is no fabricated property-history chart.
- **Causal housing capture or paired regional elasticities:** not identified. Published estimates with different geographies/horizons are documented separately; sliders remain hypothetical. No causal percentage or unsupported confidence interval is reported.
- **Complete retail purchaser-price decomposition:** detailed domestic/import-use and margin inputs were not acquired. The permitted official-multiplier fallback is implemented, with a separately labelled hypothetical retail calculator. No brand-specific domestic-content claim is made.
- **Full issuer-by-issuer statement simulation:** the audit covers 24 product-family/jurisdiction records across 11 issuer brands, but only six matched Tangerine variants have sufficient verified inputs to enable simulation. Fees, exact daily billing cycles and personalized transaction histories remain outside scope.
- **Original reporting and public publication:** interview plans are supplied; no interviews, external messages or public deployment were performed. User-provided course and conversation materials remain in the private handoff.

These are evidence limitations, not hidden implementation placeholders. Each product remains useful within its documented scope.
