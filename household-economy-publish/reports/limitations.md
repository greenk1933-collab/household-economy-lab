# Limitations and reporting still required

- This is an issuer-informed, tested pilot, not an exact amortization disclosure or statement reconstruction. New spending, annual/transfer fees, instalments, insurance, grace-period recapture, default repricing, arrears and account-specific overrides are excluded.
- Raw downloading encountered DNS restrictions initially. Root-network download recovered most documents. BMO timed out; its PDF was verified through the web viewer. RBC's cash-back update URL returned a tiny redirect-only response, although official web-indexed content showed an October 2026 APR change. Neither gap is presented as an acquired full document; the source register tracks usable files.
- Product-level APR and minimum coverage is narrower than allocation coverage. The simulator intentionally excludes unmatched institutions. National Bank's Quebec update remains unresolved; do not infer that the base document defines current lawful treatment.
- Three Tangerine products share one contract. Six jurisdiction records do not mean six independent policy observations. Family-level rows cannot be presented as card counts.
- Actual daily dates, unpaid fee treatment, within-rate tie breaking, legacy accounts and subsequent personalized amendments need issuer confirmation. Simulation requires already-interest-bearing purchases and excludes new transfer fees. World Elite recurring annual fees are omitted.
- Before publication: seek issuer responses, legal confirmation of national and Quebec implementation dates, account-specific anonymized statements with authorization, and independent verification of simulated statements. No private consumer data was acquired.
- No causal claim is made about Budget 2026, lender profits or household behaviour; the example is deterministic arithmetic under stated assumptions.


# Housing limits and remaining reporting

- No credible untreated counterfactual, matched transaction panel or local demand/supply identification is available. Obtain repeated original developer sheets, exact unit IDs, comparable incentives/tax basis, purchaser closing statements with consent, and an appropriate control group before estimating causal rebate capture.
- NHPI is a published price index, not dollar transaction microdata. Geographic definitions differ from the construction series; the UI does not automatically join incompatible areas. Starts and completions are flows, while under-construction inventory is a stock, not unsold completed inventory.
- The 48 builder rows describe model/elevation offers, not 48 sold homes. Decor allowances are not cash savings. Filename and printed price-sheet dates disagree; the printed date controls and the conflict is retained.
- Local elasticity intervals are unavailable. Published historical city supply and national demand estimates do not establish current new-home response to this policy. No parametric standard errors or causal regression are manufactured.
- Statutory calculator scope, dates, ordinary eligibility and first-time conditions must be certified by the reader. This is not a substitute for a statement of adjustments. Legal fees, MLTT administration fees, registration, development levies, title insurance and adjustments are only included if entered. Financing, time value of delayed rebates, foreign-buyer taxes, assignments, rentals, leased land and owner-building are outside the implementation.
- CRA pages were verified through the primary web viewer; some direct file downloads timed out. The downloaded consolidated regulations and Ontario administrative pages supply archived formula evidence. Source register records these differences.

Reporting next: interview CRA/Ontario on payment timing and transition rules; developers on quote/rebate conventions; purchasers on actual closing credits; economists on a credible policy identification design. No interview or outbound contact was performed without authorization.


# Limits and remaining reporting

1. The primary result is per $100 of Canadian industry output at basic prices, not per $100 at a retail checkout. A product/category retail GDP calculator needs the purchaser-price margin and domestic/import-use matrices plus a defensible product bridge. Official metadata says those matrices are available upon request; they were not acquired. The app makes this limitation prominent.
2. The retail sensitivity inputs are hypothetical, not observed import-content shares or retail margins. Its reduced two-sector decomposition omits separately identified wholesale and transport margins and is disabled for service categories.
3. The 2022 multipliers are the latest actually downloaded. The 2024 advanced IO estimates are not substituted into them. No quarterly technical matrix is asserted.
4. Industry averages cover broader activities than the consumer labels. The map is exact to named IOIC rows but is not an official complete NAPCS-to-IOIC concordance. It cannot identify a brand's ownership, supply chain, domestic content or profit destination.
5. A fixed-coefficient association is not a causal net GDP or employment gain. No induced effects, price responses, scarce-capacity displacement or alternative-spending baseline is modelled.
6. The app does not produce empirical uncertainty intervals because the source table does not supply appropriate sampling intervals for these derived category comparisons. Its source coefficients are rounded to three decimals.
7. Source inputs are public official published estimates and can be revised. There is no private consumer or business dataset.

Before publication: request national detailed margin and domestic/import-use files from Statistics Canada; reconcile model results against official simulations; obtain an authoritative NAPCS/IOIC/consumption bridge; commission a matched-category consumer-price decomposition; interview national-accounts specialists and industry sourcing experts; test substitution counterfactuals separately from gross associated production. No outreach has been sent as part of this task.
